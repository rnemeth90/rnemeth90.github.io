Not every project is pure infrastructure plumbing — this one was building a minimum viable product for a content personalization and intelligence capability. As with most MVPs, the goal was to validate the concept and get something usable in front of stakeholders quickly, rather than trying to build a fully-featured solution up front.

I don't have much more to add here in terms of proprietary detail, but from a process standpoint, this is a good example of scoping a "let's find out if this is worth investing in further" project tightly enough that it can actually ship as an MVP rather than sprawling into a much bigger, unbounded effort.
